Before designing a significant interface, run `design-research research "<the brief>" --format=<site|pwa|apk|contract_ui>` and read `.research/latest/synthesis.md`, `references.json` and the screenshots.

Use the references as research material: take patterns that several of them share (type, spacing, colour, motion rhythm), and respect what the format can hold (`transposable_to_format`). The traits are measurements; art direction, tone and why a reference works are for you to judge from the pictures. Do not copy a site's identity, layout, content, assets or distinctive implementation.
