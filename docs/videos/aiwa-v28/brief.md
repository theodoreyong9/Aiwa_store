# v28 — the two styles really fused

Every page of the film has both languages at once: the constellations (v25) are projected into the real desk (v27) — they light the wood (a blurred copy added to the plate), are reflected in it, and are screened over it. The room changes camera with each group of pages (seven moves: dolly in, orbit, low truck, crane, rack focus, push in, pull out), so no two neighbouring pages share a camera. Captions stay on their own transparent layer; one grade, grain and vignette on the whole.
Files: `plate.py` (Blender, 7 shots, `--shot 0..6`), `build.py` (film and captions layers from v25), `make.sh` (everything else). Sound: the v27 sound design, the v24 voice and music.
