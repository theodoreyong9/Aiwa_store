# Brief (stage 1)

**For** people who build with Claude Code and would rather talk than type. **Aiwa** is a widget on the phone, backed by one's own GitHub, that creates and ships by voice, with a wallet that makes the whole system an economy of production rather than of stock.

**The tension (true):** to make something with an AI today you sit at a computer, copy keys and tokens, manage git and CI, and every platform keeps your code. The film plays on that weight, then lifts it: one widget, your own account, your own repository.

**The idea, chosen among** (1) a day of someone who only talks; (2) the widget as the only object on a dark table; (3) a conversation with the super agent as the spine; (4) a descending zoom from the widget to the protocol; (5) the film as a briefing the super agent gives. **Kept: (4)** with the voice of the product as a guide: it goes from what you touch (the widget) to what carries it (sessions, agent) to what makes it fair (the wallet), and ends by saying what is not proven. It gives the order of the brief and a reason for every cut.

**Logline:** *For people who create by voice, Aiwa is the widget that builds, ships and keeps the code in your own repository, unlike platforms that keep it.* **One thing to remember:** « Tes codes restent dans ton dépôt. »

**Pre-mortem:** (a) *nobody understands the first 3 seconds*: the hook is « Un widget. Tout tient dedans. »; (b) *it looks like every product film*: the real wallet screen, the real numbers of the Yellowpaper, a sound cut on the beats of a real track; (c) *the proof is not believable*: the last scene says what is not proven yet.

**Honesty:** what is vision is marked; what is untested is named.


**Version 11:** the quality is the maximum (`extra`): four scenes of real 3D made for this film with Blender (a phone asleep and listening, a phone obeying a spoken instruction with the widget on its screen, Earth and Mars with the delay of the light, glasses with the widget in front of the lens), the wallet shown with a real capture of its screen, every cut on a beat of the track, a master at crf 18. Nothing of the film v9 is reused.
