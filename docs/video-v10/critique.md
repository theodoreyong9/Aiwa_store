# Critique (stage 6): honest, and NOT independent

*First version of this film: I redrew everything with simple cards and a poor drawing of glasses, and threw away what the film v9 already did much better (3D phones, 3D planets, 3D glasses, the payment screens, the production curve). This version is a montage of v9's best scenes and the new ones.*

A fresh pair of eyes was not used: I judged my own film from contact sheets (every 3 s) and from the checks, which is the weak form of this stage. Scores 1 to 5:

| Criterion | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 4 | « Un widget. Tout tient dedans. » is a promise in two seconds; no logo |
| Clarity of the message | 4 | one idea per shot, 7 words at most on a card; the last line repeats the one thing to remember |
| Pace and variety | 4 | cuts on every beat of the track, 1 to 4 bars; 3D shots, UI scenes and diagrams alternate |
| Movement in every shot | 4 | everything enters, counts, draws or scrolls; the camera moves are slight |
| Legibility | 4 | big type; some small captions (the tags) are hard to read on a phone |
| Art direction | 4 | one palette, one type, one motion language |
| Sound and sync | 4 by the numbers | cuts on the beats (the grid was measured on the track), -13.7 LUFS; **I did not listen to it** |
| Honesty | 5 | examples and visions are marked, the Yellowpaper figures are quoted, the last scene says what is not proven |
| Call to action | 3 | the address is shown small at the very end |

What would raise it: the person's own screen recordings of the widget (the biggest gap: no footage of a phone), a second voice take with a more natural engine, a vertical 9:16 cut, and a critique by someone who has not seen the code.

**Known limits of this montage:** the 3D shots come from the v9 film (compressed at crf 29 there), so they are softer than the new scenes; some scenes of v9 are cut short to fit the bars (their last animations are lost); the on-screen words of the v9 scenes are those of v9 (e.g. « Après la voix, le regard » on the glasses), not always the words of the voice; the footage of a real phone is still missing.
