# Style sheet

- **Palette** (60/30/10): ground #090912 (dark violet-black), support #1e1e2a cards, accents: violet #7b61ff (agent, protocol), teal #26c6b0 (loops, flows), orange #cc785c (Aiwa), green #58d39b (confirmation, « c'est fait »).
- **Type:** Liberation Sans (Arial-like), 800 for the message, 500 to 700 for support; 7 words at most per card.
- **Motion:** ease-out, entrances 0.35 to 0.7 s, stagger of one beat (0.577 s) inside a shot; every shot moves; a camera move under 8 %.
- **Rhythm:** every cut falls on a beat of the track (104 bpm, bar = 2.308 s); shots of one to four bars; the loud part of the track (78 to 97 s) carries the wallet.
- **Sound:** « The Complex » (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0) in front, ducked lightly under the voice; the voice is short lines, none over the quietest bar (the pause before the super agent is spoken softly, then the music enters); silence on the last bar.
- **Grade:** grain 0.06, vignette 0.5, the same on every shot.
